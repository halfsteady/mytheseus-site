Change it by asking.

  1. 1

    Ask

    On your app’s Coding page, say what you’d like to change, in your own words. The first time, a guide takes you through it.

  2. 2

    Try

    An AI coding agent makes the change. When it’s ready, press Try it to see it on your device.

  3. 3

    Keep

    Keep it, Change something or Put it back. The people you share with only get what you keep.

Easy to share.

Make a web app that works on iPhone, Android and computers, or a native Android app. Then choose who gets it. They get the versions you keep, without the coding controls.

  • A link

    Send a web app’s link or QR code. It works until you turn it off, unless you set a limit.

  • A Hub

    Put a set of web and Android apps behind one link, for family, friends or a team.

  • The public gallery

    List an app for anyone to find. Signed-in people can copy it and make it their own, unless you turn copies off.

  • A team

    Give an app to a team, and its members can change it with you.

Good to know.

  • Every change is a trial until you keep it, and you can put the previous version back.
  • You hear when a change is ready to try. On iPhone, add the web app to the Home Screen first.
  • Upload a picture for your app’s icon, or choose from AI-drawn ideas.
  • Web apps can show live data from outside tools, like metrics from Grafana.
  • Activity is counted on the Mytheseus server, with no third-party scripts. Shared web apps tell people before sending launch reports, which they can stop. Activity never includes what people type.
  • Google sign-in, and read-only Google Calendar for web apps, are being tested.

Some of these depend on how a Mytheseus server is set up: its operator switches on emailed sign-in codes, AI icon ideas and connections to outside tools.

Why Mytheseus?

Like the ship of Theseus, an app can change a piece at a time and still be yours. Mytheseus is an experiment in software that adapts to real needs through small, reviewable steps.
